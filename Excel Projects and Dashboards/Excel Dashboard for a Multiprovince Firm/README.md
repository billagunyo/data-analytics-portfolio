#Multi-Province Store Sales Dashboard

## Overview
This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales performance across multiple cities, branches, product lines, and customer demographics. It enables stakeholders to monitor key metrics, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions to optimize revenue and operational efficiency.

## Data Sources
- **Sales Transactions**: Aggregated from stores in Montreal, Toronto, and Vancouver.
- **Customer Demographics**: Includes age group and gender segmentation.
- **Product Lines**: Fashion, Food and Beverages, Electronic Accessories.

## Key Metrics
- **COGs (Cost of Goods Sold)**: $13,717,139.06
- **Shipping Cost**: $1,049,001.70
- **Total Revenue by Gender**:
  - Male: $6,705,105.02
  - Female: $6,901,700.19
- **Age Group Distribution**:
  - Age 31–45: 49.98% (dominant segment)

## Visual Components

### 1. Revenue & Profit Analysis
- **Bar Chart: Total Revenue by Product Line**
- **Bar Chart: Average Profits by Product Line**
  - Highlights profitability across categories.

### 2. Demographic Insights
- **Donut Chart: Age Group Distribution**
- **Gender-Based Revenue Comparison**
  - Supports targeted marketing and staffing strategies.

### 3. Trend Analysis
- **Line Chart: Time-Series Metrics**
  - Tracks performance over time for forecasting and anomaly detection.

### 4. Cost Breakdown
- **Circular Gauges**:
  - COGs and Shipping Costs visualized for quick reference.

### 5. Interactive Filters
- **Slicers**:
  - City: Montreal, Toronto, Vancouver
  - Branch: Branch1, Branch2, Branch3
  - Age Group: 18–30, 31–45, 46–65
  - Product Line: Electronic Accessories, Fashion, Food and Beverages

## Use Cases
- **Executive Review**: High-level performance summary across regions.
- **Branch Management**: Compare branch-level metrics for staffing and inventory decisions.
- **Marketing Strategy**: Align campaigns with dominant age and gender segments.
- **Product Strategy**: Prioritize high-revenue and high-profit product lines.
