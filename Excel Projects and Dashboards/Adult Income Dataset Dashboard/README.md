# Income Dashboard

## Overview
This dashboard presents a multidimensional analysis of income distribution across demographic, educational, and employment factors. It is designed to support researchers, policymakers, and analysts in identifying income disparities and trends across population segments.

## Data Sources
- **Demographic Data**: Gender, age, marital status, and education level
- **Employment Data**: Work class, employment category, and work hours
- **Engagement Metrics**: Shares, likes, comments, and views

## Key Engagement Metrics
- **Shares**: 1.17M  
- **Likes**: 29.34M  
- **Comments**: 22.58M  
- **Views**: 1,202.59M  

## Visual Components

### 1. Educational Level Panel
- **Insight**: 55.1% of individuals with a bachelor's degree earn more than $50,000.
- **Use Case**: Supports education policy and workforce development planning.

### 2. Income by Gender
- **Insight**: 43% of women earn more than $50,000.
- **Use Case**: Highlights gender disparities for equity-focused initiatives.

### 3. Income & Work Hours by Years of Schooling
- **Line Chart**: Correlates years of schooling with income and work hours.
- **Use Case**: Evaluates ROI of education in labor economics.

### 4. Income by Work Class
- **Bar Chart**: Income distribution across Private, Self-employed, Government, etc.
- **Use Case**: Assesses sector-based income trends.

### 5. Income by Marital Status
- **Bar Chart**: Income comparison across Married, Single, Divorced, etc.
- **Use Case**: Supports social policy and household income studies.

### 6. Employment Category
- **Pie Chart**: Employment category distribution.
- **Highlighted Insight**: 25.5% of people fall into a specific category.
- **Use Case**: Workforce segmentation and labor market analysis.

### 7. Sidebar Navigation
- **Filters**: Shares, Education, Work Class, Marital Status, Employment, Gender, Income
- **Use Case**: Enables targeted exploration of income factors.

## Use Cases
- **Policy Development**: Inform education, labor, and gender equity policies.
- **Academic Research**: Support socioeconomic studies and income modeling.
- **Public Engagement**: Communicate income insights through high-engagement metrics.
